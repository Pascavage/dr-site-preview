Cap table & equity

Core Six capabilities

Your cap table is calculated from the transactions underneath it, never typed over. When someone asks why a holder owns what they own, the answer is a documented chain of events.

Cap table

  • Live table derived from underlying transactions
  • Configurable templates so each audience sees the right view
  • Version snapshots — take one before a raise or board meeting, and view the table as of that moment
  • One click to an investor-ready PDF

Transactions

  • Issuance, transfer, cancellation, conversion, exercise, repurchase
  • Draft → final → voided, so work in progress never touches live numbers
  • Commit drafts one at a time, in a batch, or all on a round
  • CSV import with a preview step before anything commits

Funding rounds

  • Build a round with per-investor allocations, review it whole, then commit
  • New issuance and pool allocation
  • Price by valuation or by price per share
  • Close and reopen as terms move

Classes & convertibles

  • Voting rights, anti-dilution (broad, narrow, full ratchet), participation (non-participating, full, capped)
  • A sensible default set can be seeded
  • SAFEs, convertible notes and KISS instruments

Grants & vesting

  • Grants issued individually or in batch; exercise and cancellation
  • Time, milestone and hybrid schedules; monthly, quarterly or annual
  • Single- and double-trigger acceleration
  • Per-row overrides that clear back to "inherits default"

Departures & modeling

  • Termination & forfeiture with a preview before you commit, reversible if entered in error
  • What-if modeling: pre-money, raise, new class, pool refresh, convertible inclusion
  • Exit waterfall at any valuation
  • Save scenarios and compare two side by side

Diligence

Core

Checklists

  • Built from templates you control
  • Status per item: pending, complete, not applicable, not available
  • Notes on any item

Evidence

  • Attach documents from your library, or upload straight against an item
  • AI review checks an attached document against what the item is asking for

Controlled sharing

  • Diligence contacts and rooms for external parties
  • Room structure derives from your checklist categories

Data rooms

Admin only

Built on the assumption that a shared link will be forwarded, a screenshot will be taken, and someone will need to be cut off mid-session.

Access control

  • Each recipient gets a personal link
  • Access requires a one-time 6-digit code — a forwarded link is useless to anyone else
  • Expiry dates; revocation takes effect immediately, mid-session

Document protection

  • Every document watermarked with the viewer's identity, applied server-side
  • Streamed through the application — guests never receive a link into your drive
  • Downloads off by default; controllable room-wide and per guest

Visibility & lifecycle

  • Access log: who opened which document, and when
  • Delivery tracking on invitations
  • Revoke, close, archive, delete and restore

Guest experience

  • Folder browsing, search and sort
  • Pinned documents
  • Full-screen in-browser PDF viewer — nothing to download or install

Financials

New

Configuration

  • Monthly, quarterly or annual reporting
  • Mark each statement type required or optional
  • Share-price decimal precision

Statement types

  • P&L, balance sheet, cash flow
  • Cap table, AR aging, bank statement
  • Supporting schedules

Periods

  • Reporting periods open themselves on schedule
  • Backfill tool for historical gaps
  • Per-period slots showing what's filed and what's outstanding

Board management

New

Run the document side of a board meeting inside Diligence Ready instead of over email — with secure, watermarked materials and a record of who actually read the package.

Boards & members

  • Multiple boards per company
  • Seven roles from chair to observer
  • Start dates, term ends, and a flag when a term is ending soon

Meetings

  • Schedule against a board, with location and agenda detail
  • Multiple documents in the package and the minutes, reorderable
  • Team-only notes — visible to your staff, never to invitees
  • RSVP tracking

Invitee portal

  • Personal secure link plus a one-time code — no account, no password
  • Materials watermarked with the member's identity
  • Session persists so they aren't re-prompted while reading
  • Downloads off unless you enable them

Engagement

  • Per-document view tracking — know who read the package before the meeting
  • Daily digest email to admins
Note

Board invitees can currently open PDF materials in the portal. Word and Excel files can be attached today; in-portal viewing for those formats is coming shortly.

Stakeholders & investor relations

Records

  • Individuals, entities, trusts and funds
  • Archive and restore rather than destructive deletion

Investor portal

  • Invite a stakeholder to see their own holdings
  • Read-only — nothing they can alter

Communication

  • Direct chat between company and stakeholders, with unread tracking
  • Investors request agreement documents; requests land in an inbox you fulfil or dismiss

Files & storage

Your storage, not ours

  • Documents live in your own Google Drive or OneDrive
  • Connected once, at Settings → Integrations
  • One provider at a time

Library

  • Files link to stakeholders, transactions, diligence items, statements and board materials
  • Resumable uploads for large documents
  • Duplicate detection

AI assistant

Ask

  • Answers questions across stakeholders, classes, rounds and transactions
  • Scoped to your company alone

Draft

  • Prepares stakeholders, security classes and funding rounds
  • Creates draft transactions that don't touch the cap table until a person commits them
  • Extracts terms from a signed agreement

Limits

  • Monthly allowance by plan
  • Unavailable inside read-only advisory sessions

Team, access & Trusted Advisors

Roles

  • Admin, editor, investor view, counsel
  • Fixed by design — a permissions model you can't misconfigure won't leak
  • A reference chart of exactly what each role can do

Membership

  • Email invitations, resend, revoke
  • Pause a member without losing their history
  • One login across multiple companies, with a switcher

Trusted Advisors

  • Access grants for accountants and fractional CFOs: requested, approved by you, time-limited, revocable
  • Trusted Advisor firms get their own dashboard and client work queue
  • Adviser activity appears in your audit log like anyone else's

Security & trust

Specific controls rather than reassuring language. We'd rather tell you exactly what is in place and let you judge it.

Company isolation
Row-level isolation in the database between companies
Audit log
Who did what and when, visible to you in settings
External access
One-time codes valid 15 minutes, 5 attempts, then a temporary lock
Watermarking
Server-side identity watermark on every externally shared document
Revocation
Effective on the next document opened, not at next sign-in
Multi-factor
Authenticator app, with backup codes
Credentials
Encrypted at rest, with key rotation support
Your data
Export your records; delete your account, with safeguards against removing a company's last administrator
