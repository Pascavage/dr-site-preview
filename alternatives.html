Who this is for

Founders and company leaders, and the CFOs, advisors, and accountants beside them.

Diligence Ready is built for companies that want a cap table connected to ongoing financial evidence and diligence readiness, and for the practitioners who carry more than one company's records at once. It may not be the right fit for every situation, which is exactly why the criteria below matter more than a single verdict.

Comparison criteria

What actually differs between cap table tools.

Not every provider publishes the same information, so treat gaps as gaps, not as a point in anyone's favor.

①

Workflow coverage

Does the tool stop at the cap table, or does it also cover scenario modeling, financial evidence, and diligence sharing?

②

Who it's built for

Single company or multi-client. Founder-facing or built around a practitioner's book of clients.

③

Storage & access model

Where documents live, and how access and sharing are handled once a diligence request arrives.

Before you switch

Questions worth asking before you commit to a change.

  • ✓ What does migrating the existing cap table actually involve?
  • ✓ Do you need more than a cap table, such as scenario modeling or a diligence room?
  • ✓ Who else at the company or among your advisors will use it day to day?
  • ✓ Does the provider publish current pricing and terms you can verify yourself?
Migration considerations

Moving a cap table is not just an export and an import.

Historical transactions, agreements, and prior versions all need to carry over accurately for the new record to be trustworthy. Ask any provider you're considering, including us, exactly how that history is handled before you commit.

A wider decision than the cap table alone

A cap table decision and a readiness decision aren't always the same choice.

Some tools solve narrowly for keeping share counts accurate. Diligence Ready connects that record to financial evidence and diligence documents on an ongoing basis, so readiness doesn't depend on a separate assembly step later. Whether that connected workflow matters to you is worth deciding on its own, separate from which cap table tool has which feature.
