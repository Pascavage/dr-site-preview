Questions, answered

How the assistant works.

What can the AI assistant do?

It answers questions about your cap table and transactions in plain language, models what-if scenarios, extracts terms from SAFEs and convertible notes, and drafts investor updates. Anything that would change your records is proposed as a reviewable action, not executed automatically.

Does the AI change my cap table automatically?

No. The assistant proposes — you approve. Every action that would mutate your data is presented for review with the exact changes shown, and nothing is committed until you sign off. There are no surprise edits.

What AI model is it built on?

It's built on a leading frontier large language model, chosen for careful reasoning over your numbers rather than generic chat. The emphasis is on nuance and accuracy — not bullet-point summaries that sound confident and get the math wrong.

Can it read my SAFEs and convertible notes?

Yes. Upload an agreement and the assistant extracts the key terms — amount, valuation cap, discount, and dates — and drafts the corresponding transaction for you to review side by side with the source document before committing.

Is my data used to train AI models?

Your company's data is yours. It is used to answer your questions and do your work — not to train public models. Sensitive records stay within your account and your chosen storage.
