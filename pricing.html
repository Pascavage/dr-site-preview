Questions, answered

Pricing, in plain terms.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Founders is free forever: 3 users (internal and investor), the full cap table, 150 AI credits a month, one data room (3 guests), one board (5 invitees), and 1 scenario.

Is there a free data room for startups?

Yes. On Diligence Ready, the data room is part of your plan, not a separate charge. The Founders plan is free forever and includes 1 data room with 3 guests, the full cap table, and 3 users. There are no per-page data room fees, and your files stay in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Guests view documents through the app, watermarked with their identity, and never get a link into your Drive. Need more? Standard is $99/mo for up to 10 users and includes up to 5 data rooms with 25 guests. See how the diligence room works.

How much does Diligence Ready cost?

Founders is $0 forever. Standard is $99/mo for up to 10 users. Growth ($250/mo, 25 users) and Scale ($500/mo, 100 users) are coming soon. Annual billing is two months free.

Do you charge per seat?

No per-seat fees. There's no line item for an extra person inside your tier. Each tier includes a set number of internal and investor users together — 3 on Founders, 10 on Standard, 25 on Growth, 100 on Scale — so adding an investor doesn't add to the bill until you outgrow the tier.

How much does the Diligence Ready data room cost?

On Diligence Ready, the data room is part of your plan, not a separate charge. Founders is free and includes 1 data room with 3 guests. Standard includes up to 5 data rooms with 25 guests. Growth and Scale, both coming soon, include unlimited data rooms. Page count never changes the price. Current prices for each plan are listed at the top of this page.

Do you charge per page for the data room?

No. Every plan includes data rooms at a flat plan price: 1 room with 3 guests on Founders, up to 5 rooms with 25 guests on Standard. Nothing is charged by the page, and your files stay in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Typical per-page data rooms charge by document volume.

Where do my files live?

In your own Google Drive or OneDrive, on any plan. Diligence Ready organizes and references the documents there; storage permissions stay governed by your Google or Microsoft account and your own admin settings. Guests view documents through the app, watermarked with their identity, and never get a link into your Drive. More on the features page.

How do AI credits work?

Every plan includes monthly credits for AI features like cap-table questions and reading signed documents into your cap table. Credits reset monthly. If you need more, credit packs start at $5 and get cheaper per credit the more you buy. Packs never expire.

What's the annual discount?

Two months free: pay ten months for twelve. Standard works out to $82.50/mo, billed as $990/year.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, upgrade in a few clicks through Stripe Checkout, no sales call. Manage invoices and plan changes in the billing portal.
