The cost of last-minute prep

Assembling records under a deadline costs more than the time it takes.

Reconstructing a cap table, chasing down old agreements, and pulling financials together once conversations have already started competes directly with the raise itself, and a rushed record is more likely to have gaps that surface later.

A readiness model kept current beforehand avoids that tradeoff entirely.

A practical readiness model

Maintain records. Model decisions. Assemble diligence. Share securely.

The same four stages, run continuously rather than compressed into the weeks before a raise.
