Trust at the architecture level

Built so investors can trust the numbers and you can sleep.

Tier-isolated by construction

Every login is locked to a single tier in the database. A customer account can’t reach platform admin, even if compromised, because the identity itself is walled off.

Row-level security

Enforced at the database, not just the app. Each investor sees only their own data. Multi-tenant isolation that holds up under scrutiny.

Audit log on everything

Every change attributed and timestamped. Read-only support sessions let advisors look without touching. Soft-archive means an accidental delete can’t break history.

Bring your own storage

Files live in your Google Drive or OneDrive, your security policies, your backups, your permissions. We don’t lock your documents in our cloud.

AI with a human in the loop

The assistant proposes; you approve. No data is mutated without your sign-off, and every AI action lands in the same audit trail.

Ready for the room

A document trail an acquirer can follow, per-investor scoping, and read-only support sessions. SOC 2 is on the roadmap.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How are advisors billed?

They aren’t. Diligence Ready is free for trusted advisors. Your clients pay for their own usage and any upgrades, and every company starts free. If your firm manages a lot of clients, talk to us about onboarding and a co-branded portal.

Who counts as a trusted advisor?

Fractional CFOs, accounting firms, law firms, angel groups and PE firms. Anyone who supports more than one company’s cap table, finances or diligence.

What can I see inside a client’s workspace?

Only what the client grants. Access can be read-only or hands-on, and time-limited if they prefer. Every action you take is logged and attributed to you.

Can my firm put its own brand on it?

Yes. Firms that carry a lot of clients can set up a co-branded portal, so clients see your name alongside ours. Talk to us and we’ll help you get set up.

Where are client documents stored?

In each company’s own Google Drive or OneDrive. Diligence Ready links and organizes the files where the room can find them, but their security policies, backups, and permissions stay theirs.
