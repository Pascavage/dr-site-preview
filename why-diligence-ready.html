Questions, answered

Why run this way.

What does Diligence Ready actually do?

It runs the four things a company has to be able to show: ownership, financials, the board, and the diligence room. The cap table is the live ledger everything keys off, scenario modeling runs on it, reporting periods keep the financials current, and the room keeps the documents and the gaps in view.

Why run this way when nobody is raising?

Because readiness can't be assembled on demand. A round, an acquisition, a loan, or an audit arrives on someone else's schedule. Companies that keep ownership, financials, and documents current as a daily habit meet it from a standing start — everyone else assembles the answer under a deadline.

How does pricing work?

Founders is free forever with 3 users. Standard is $99/mo for 10. Each tier counts internal and investor users together, so there's no line item for an extra person inside your tier. Annual billing is two months free.

Can I migrate from my current tool or spreadsheet?

Yes. You can import an existing cap table from a spreadsheet and start from a clean, defensible ledger, with every line linked to the agreement behind it.

Is it built for Trusted Advisors: advisors, firms, and investors?

Yes. Fractional CFOs, accounting and law firms, angel groups and PE firms all get one dashboard across every company they carry, with gated drill-down, audited access, and a co-branded portal. The relationship stays yours.
