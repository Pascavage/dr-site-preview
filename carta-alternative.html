Diligence readiness

Where Diligence Ready's approach differs, and where it has limits.

Diligence Ready connects the cap table to an ongoing diligence checklist and a shareable data room, built from documents kept current in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Diligence Ready is a newer, narrower platform than Carta's broader equity management suite, and doesn't offer 409A valuations, fund administration, or the add-on services Carta lists on its pricing page. Whether that tradeoff makes sense depends on what your company actually needs.

Migration questions

Ask these before moving a cap table off Carta or anywhere else.

  • ✓ How will historical transactions and prior versions transfer?
  • ✓ Do you rely on Carta services, like 409A valuations, that aren't part of Diligence Ready?
  • ✓ Who needs to review the migrated cap table before you rely on it?

We don't claim migration between any two providers goes smoothly by default. Plan for a review step regardless of which platform you choose.
