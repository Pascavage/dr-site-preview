Why storage control matters

Your documents already have a home. This doesn't replace it.

Corporate, financial, and legal documents already live in Google Drive or OneDrive, under permissions and policies your team set up. A diligence workflow that requires copying everything into a separate system adds a second place to manage and a second set of permissions to keep straight.

Organizing the workflow around your existing storage keeps one home for the documents, not two.

Google Drive and OneDrive, connected separately.

Organization & mapping

Documents mapped to categories, not copied into a new system.

Once connected, documents in your storage are organized against the categories your diligence workflow tracks, cap table, financials, governance, IP, and more, so the diligence checklist and the room draw from the same connected files.

Permissions boundary

Your storage permissions stay yours to manage.

The connection lets Diligence Ready organize and reference documents in your Google Drive or OneDrive. Underlying storage permissions and account administration remain governed by Google or Microsoft and your own IT or admin settings.

From connected storage to a room

The diligence room draws from the same connected files.

When you're ready to share, the diligence room is built from the documents already organized through your connected storage, with access set per person for whoever you invite in. Data rooms come with your plan, with no per-page fees, and our guide to virtual data room pricing explains how other pricing models compare.
