FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Can I bring my cap table over from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload the spreadsheet or your signed agreements and the assistant reads them in. You review every entry before it’s saved, and each one stays linked to the document behind it.

What do investors see?

Only what you share. Each investor gets a read-only view of their own holdings, and a data room shows just the files you pick, with a record of who opened what.

How much does Diligence Ready cost?

Founders is free forever for up to 3 users. Standard is $99/month for up to 10 users. Growth ($250/month) and Scale ($500/month) are coming soon. Annual billing is two months free. See full pricing.

Can I model a priced round before I commit to it?

Yes. Scenario modeling runs on your live cap table for a priced round, an option pool refresh, convertible conversions or an exit waterfall. It shows exactly who is diluted and who gets paid, to four decimals. Nothing is written to the ledger until you decide to commit it.

Where are my documents stored?

In your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Diligence Ready links and organizes the files where the room can find them, but your security policies, backups, and permissions stay yours.
