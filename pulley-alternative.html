Pulley shutdown, as of October 9, 2026

Pulley is shutting down on December 8, 2026.

Pulley's own notice says it will cease all operations and services on December 8, 2026.A The dates below come from Pulley's shutdown FAQ, dated September 15, 2026.B Confirm the details that apply to your account with Pulley directly.

  • ✓November 30, 2026: the date in Pulley's FAQ for opting in to the assisted migration to Carta that Pulley arranged.B
  • ✓December 8, 2026: Pulley says the Pulley app experience will no longer be accessible, and its team will no longer be available to support your organization.B
  • ✓January 31, 2027: Pulley says your data stays available in a limited format through the Pulley app until this date.B

What Pulley users can do now

Export your records first, whichever provider you choose next. Pulley's help center lists these exports:C

  • ✓Cap table: Download Cap Table, with All Time checked, gives the full history as an Excel file.
  • ✓Documents: Download All in Pulley's Data Room gives one ZIP file, with your folders kept.
  • ✓Share certificates: Download All Certificates, if you issued them through Pulley.
  • ✓Reports: under Company, then Reports, such as Granted Securities, Vesting Details, Exercised Options and Cancelled Securities.

Pulley says it can't assist a migration to any provider other than Carta, so a move anywhere else starts from these exports.B Check the rebuilt cap table against your signed documents before you rely on it.

Where Diligence Ready fits

You can import an existing cap table from a spreadsheet (CSV imports show a preview step before anything commits) and keep your documents in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Founders is free for up to 3 users, and Standard is $99/mo for up to 10 users. We don't promise a one-step move from Pulley's export, so plan a review step. Diligence Ready is not affiliated with Pulley or Carta.

Connected diligence

Where Diligence Ready's approach differs, and where it has limits.

Diligence Ready connects the cap table to an ongoing diligence checklist and a shareable data room, built from documents kept current in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Pulley's help center describes a Pulley Data Room for company documents.C We could not confirm from Pulley's public pages whether it connects to an ongoing diligence checklist, so we don't assume either way. Diligence Ready is a newer, narrower platform overall, and doesn't publish independent security certifications the way Pulley states it does. Whether either tradeoff matters depends on what your company needs.

Migration questions

Ask these before moving a cap table off Pulley or anywhere else.

  • ✓ How will historical transactions and prior versions transfer?
  • ✓ Do you rely on Pulley features, like managed services or specific integrations, that aren't part of Diligence Ready?
  • ✓ Who needs to review the migrated cap table before you rely on it?

We don't claim migration between any two providers goes smoothly by default. Plan for a review step regardless of which platform you choose.
